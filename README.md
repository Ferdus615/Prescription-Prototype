# Doctor Portal prescription module - clickable design prototype

Plain HTML + CSS + vanilla JS. No build, no network, no framework. All state is in memory (reload resets it). All data is mock and **not clinically verified** (brands, strengths and generics are illustrative).

## How to open

Double-click `index.html`. Files: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `data.js` (all mock data), `app.js` (state + render functions).
Desktop-first (laptop); usable at tablet width. Bangla text renders via the system font stack (falls back to Noto Sans Bengali / Nirmala UI).
Keyboard: Tab through fields; in medicine search use Up/Down + Enter; press `/` to jump to medicine search; Esc closes pop-ups.

## Screens and what they demonstrate

1. **Appointments** - today's list (21 Oct 2026) with returning / first-visit / family tags. "Open consultation" starts the writer.
2. **Consultation + Rx writer** - header (doctor, BMDC, chamber, date, serial), patient strip (masked phone, relation, allergy chip, editable vitals, derived BMI).
   - Left: note sections from `prescription_design.md` section 3 (C/C, K/C/O, D/H, P/H, F/H, O/E, O/I, advised tests, Dx with Provisional/Differential/Final, Advice Bangla+English, Follow-up). Collapse, hide, or reorder via **Customise sections**; **Manage sections** (PHAR-1703): remove any section (built-in too, × on its header) and restore it; rename any section; reorder; add your own (also by typing a name in the dashed "+ New section" field under the notes); and per section manage its **saved notes** (add, edit in place, delete; PHAR-1692). To add what you wrote to your list: click **☆ Save to list** next to the field, click **☆** on any note chip (★ = in your list, click again to take it off), or pick **"☆ Add … and save to my notes"** in the dropdown. Saved entries are suggested next time and found by searching. **Follow-up**: after N Days / Weeks / Months with the date derived, plus slot booking and a reminder SMS (PHAR-1699).
   - **Note suggestions**: focus or type in any section; the dropdown ranks the doctor's own entries (tagged recent / frequent / used before) above common phrases; click or Enter inserts a chip. Advice lines without Bangla get a "Bangla text needed for print" hint.
   - Right: medicine search over a mock catalogue (Enter adds; last row = free text), per-line dose pattern (morning/noon/night, every N hours, SOS), amount + unit, food, route, duration or Continue, auto-calculated dispense quantity, **multiple instructions per medicine** (type + Enter or pick saved formats; each shows as a removable chip, all print on the prescription; "Save typed text as my format"), caution, reorder/collapse/remove.
   - **Quick add**: one-click chips above the medicine list. Each is a single-medicine preset with its full dose, food, duration and instructions. Add one with the **☆** button on any medicine line (free-text lines excluded); remove one with the **×** on its chip. **Manage** (end of the chip row) opens the Manage quick add screen: list in chip order with reorder ↑↓, Edit, Delete and **+ New**, and an edit form (medicine, dose, food, route, duration or Continue, instructions) with a live preview of the chip. Limit 10 chips. Feature ticket: PHAR-1755 (builds on PHAR-1696 presets).
   - **Dosage steps / tapering (PHAR-1716)**: under each medicine line, **+ Add dose step** turns the line into numbered steps run in order (tapering, loading dose then maintenance, any dose change over time). Each step has its own dose type, morning/noon/night or interval, amount, and duration, plus its calendar range. Unit, food, route, instructions and caution belong to the whole line. Only the last step can be **Continue**. Removing steps down to one turns the line back into a normal single dose. **Auto taper…** builds a step-down from a start amount, an amount to reduce by, a number of days per step and a last amount, and shows the steps and quantity before you apply. **Start**: Today / After N days / On a date. This moves the step dates and prints "… থেকে শুরু করবেন" but does not change the quantity. The dispense quantity is the sum of all steps. The printed sheet and patient view show one drug with numbered steps ("১) … ৫ দিন, তারপর / ২) …"). Steps are kept by quick add, templates, Save as template, issue, and Continue from previous (the start date is not copied). The Manage quick add screen shows a taper's steps read-only.
     - Try: Quick add **Cortan 5** (8→6→4→2 tab, 5 days each = 100 tablets), or Apply template → **Asthma flare (steroid taper)**. On any line, set Start = After 5 days and open Preview.
   - **Infusion + sliding scale (PHAR-1717)**: on an IV or SC line an **Infusion** box takes volume (ml/L), rate (drops/min or ml/hr) and over N hours; it prints "১০০০ মিলি · প্রতি মিনিটে ২০ ফোঁটা · ৮ ঘণ্টায় (1000 ml at 20 drops/min over 8 h)". **+ Sliding scale** adds condition/dose rows (Bangla OK) printed as a small table on the sheet and patient view. Empty rows block Complete with a field-level error. Quantity for these lines is typed by the doctor, not computed.
     - Try: Quick add **Normal Saline 1000 ml** and **Humulin sliding scale**, then Preview. Add an empty row and press Complete to see the errors.
   - **Stop / Continue previous (PHAR-1718)**: **+ Stop a medicine** (pick from the patient's current medicines, search the catalogue or type any name, optional reason) adds a STOP line: prints "বন্ধ করুন / STOP: ...", never dispensed, not warning-checked, not carried into Continue from previous. **+ Continue previous medicines** lists only prescriptions you can read (your own; another doctor's only if the patient shares it, none shared in the demo) and copies a line with its schedule, editable, tagged "চলবে / Continue", dispensed like a normal line.
     - Try: Returning scenario -> Stop a medicine -> pick Napa 500 mg, reason; then Continue previous medicines -> Continue on Seclo. Family scenario shows the "not shared" message.
   - **SOS reason + side (PHAR-1721)**: SOS lines get a reason field (Bangla OK) printed after the dose. Eye drop / Ear drop / Nasal routes get a Right / Left / Both side printed next to the dose ("ডান চোখে / Right eye"). Changing to a route where side does not apply clears it.
     - Try: Quick add **Napa 500 - SOS if fever** and **Moxicol Eye Drops**, then Preview.
   - **Weight-based dosing (PHAR-1722)**: tick **Dose by weight (mg/kg)** on a line; weight comes from the vitals strip. Computes mg per dose and, for syrups, ml from the strength (rounded to 0.5), shows e.g. 12 kg x 15 mg/kg = 180 mg = 7.5 ml, stores the formula, prints it, and lets the doctor override the amount. Warns (never blocks) above the catalogue mg/kg/day maximum. No weight: error on the line and on the Weight field, and Complete is blocked.
     - Try: Family scenario (Arian, 25 kg) -> search Napa, pick Napa 120 mg/5 ml syrup -> tick Dose by weight, 15 mg/kg (15.5 ml). Change weight to 12 for 7.5 ml, clear it for the error, use 30 mg/kg for the max-dose warning.
   - **Allergy warning (PHAR-1724)**: each catalogue line is checked against the patient's allergies and any "Allergy: ..." chip in this Rx's Drug history section, by drug class or generic. A red card shows on the line (all warning styles; pop-up style also pops up; the side panel lists it). **Keep with override reason** needs a reason, stored on the line; Complete is never blocked (the confirm screen counts open ones). Free-text and stop lines are not checked. Works alongside the "Already on this medicine" duplicate warning.
     - Try: Returning scenario (Penicillin allergy) -> search Moxacil, then Clavulin (also flagged as a duplicate of Moxacil). First visit: add the chip "Allergy: Sulfa" in Drug history, then add Bactrim DS.
   - **Other orders (PHAR-1719)**: under the medicines, **Other orders** holds non-drug orders: diet, exercise, physiotherapy, procedure, dressing, nebulisation, other. Each is a card with type, English, Bangla (printed; "Bangla text needed for print" hint) and optional how often / how long. **+ Add order** shows the doctor's saved entries (star, suggested first) then common ones per type, or a custom blank card; **Save to my list** on a card adds it to the saved list (x in the modal removes it). They are not medicines: no quantity, not dispensed, not in the medicine warnings. Printed on the sheet in their own block "অন্যান্য নির্দেশনা / Other orders" and shown in the patient view. Kept by **Save as template -> Condition template**, Apply template and issue; NOT copied by Continue from previous (D2: only medicines + tests).
     - Try: Apply template -> **Hypertension follow-up** (low-salt diet + brisk walk), or + Add order -> Physiotherapy tab, then Preview.
   - **Referral, admission advice, medical certificate (PHAR-1720)**: **+ Referral** (to a speciality, a named doctor or a hospital; reason; urgency Routine / Urgent / Emergency; several allowed), **+ Admission advice** (ward type, facility, reason, urgency Immediately / Within 24 hours / Planned + date) and **+ Medical certificate** (Rest: from date + days, derived end date; or Fitness to resume: date + restrictions; optional "show diagnosis"). Each card has **Preview / print** that opens its own A4 document (letter / advice / certificate, doctor header, QR + check code, Bangla line on the certificate). Referral and admission also print on the prescription sheet in a "Referral, admission, certificate" block (the certificate there is a one-line note "printed separately"). Missing required fields (speciality or name, reason, planned date, days) show field errors and block Complete. Visit-specific: not saved into templates and not copied by Continue from previous.
     - Try: add all three, leave them empty and press Complete, fill them, Preview the sheet, then Preview / print each. Patient view -> RX-A12345-0042 (referral, certificate with diagnosis, orders) and RX-A12345-0031 (admission advice), each with **View / print**.
   - **Interaction with dosage steps**: infusion, sliding scale, SOS reason, side, allergy and stop/continue are line-level and keep working on a stepped line (printed after the steps). Weight-based dosing is single-dose only: adding a step switches it off. Quantity stays manual whenever an infusion or sliding scale is present.
   - **Pre-fill + record write-back (PHAR-1693)**: opening a consultation pre-fills K/C/O and D/H allergy chips from the patient record, D/H, P/H and F/H from your last prescription for this patient, and vitals from check-in. Chips are tagged "record" / "last visit"; a bar above the notes counts them and offers **Re-apply pre-fill** / **Clear pre-filled**. Anything you add that is not on the record (a new comorbidity or an "Allergy: ..." chip) is tagged **+ record**. Per design D8 the write-back is automatic on issue, add-only and case-insensitive: the confirm screen lists what will be added, removing a chip never deletes from the record, drafts and previews never write back, and the next visit pre-fills from this prescription.
     - Try: Returning scenario (HTN, Hypothyroidism, Penicillin, last-visit chips) -> type "Chronic kidney disease" in Comorbidity and "Allergy: Sulfa" in Drug history -> Complete to see "Patient record: will add ..." -> Confirm and issue, then open a new consultation for the same patient (chips are now "record"). First visit shows nothing to pre-fill; Mizanur (serial 5) has HTN, DM, Sulfa.
   - **Report entry (PHAR-1698)**: inside **On investigation (results)**, **Report results** takes structured rows: test, result, unit, reference range, date, flag. Picking a known test fills unit and range (mock, not clinically verified); flag is Auto (High/Low/Normal from a numeric result and a range like 12.0-15.5, <5, >1.0) or set by hand. The tests you advised at your last visit show as buttons: **Lipid profile -> enter result** adds four rows (a panel), each linked ("advised: ...") and the button shows **entered**. A row needs test, result and a date not in the future, or Complete is blocked with field errors. Results print under O/I on the sheet (value in bold, H / L marks, range, date) and show in the patient view as a table. Clinical values keep English digits.
     - Try: Returning scenario -> Report results -> Blood sugar (Fasting) -> enter 7.8 (High), then Lipid profile, then **+ Add result** with the name Hb; Preview.
   - **Health advice library (PHAR-1694)**: **Health advice library** (Advice section) lists items by category with a category filter; tick to attach to this prescription. **+ New advice item**, **Edit**, **Delete** and the up/down arrows manage the list. Bangla is mandatory (empty or no Bengali letters -> "Advice must be written in Bangla" under the field), English is optional and printed under it, with a live "how it prints" preview. Deleting also removes the line from this draft; issued prescriptions keep their text. Free-typed advice lines without Bangla now block Complete with the same field error (before, only a hint).
     - Try: Advice -> Health advice library -> + New advice item, type English only and Save (error), then add Bangla, tick "Also add it to this prescription"; Edit "Rest"; move it up; Delete one. Type "Take rest only" in Advice and press Complete.
   - **Follow-up reminder + slot (PHAR-1699)**: under the follow-up (after N days / weeks / months) a **Book follow-up slot** button opens day tabs (the due day plus the next 3; Friday shown closed) with mock times, some taken. A booked slot prints and is cleared if you change the follow-up. **Send the patient a reminder SMS** (on by default) goes 1 / 2 / 3 / 7 days before at 9:00 am; **Preview SMS (Bangla)** shows the message (patient name, doctor, date, time, a note line for "With reports", Rx number) and a simulated test send. If the send day is today or earlier the SMS goes out right after issue. For a family member it goes to the parent phone. Nothing is really sent.
     - Try: Follow-up -> Weeks -> 2 -> Book follow-up slot -> 10:20 -> Preview SMS; set Reminder to 3 days; type "With reports" in the note.
   - **Print + patient view for clinical notes (PHAR-1700)**: the printed sheet follows design section 7: header, patient line, **left panel** (K/C/O, D/H, O/E with vitals, O/I with results, Exam / Inv, Dx), **right panel** Rx, **Adv / পরামর্শ**, **F/U / পুনরায় দেখা করবেন**, signature and QR. Empty segments are omitted; the long history (P/H, F/H) is not printed unless you tick **Also print long history** in the preview (it stays in the record). The patient view now shows a **Clinical notes** block above the medicines with everything submitted (D6: nothing filtered, including history, O/E, O/I and Dx), then advice, follow-up (booked slot, reminder date) and a simulated **Download PDF**.
     - Try: Patient view -> RX-A12345-0042 (notes, results table, follow-up with reminder) or any prescription you issued in this session.
   - **Bangla digits (PHAR-1723)**: the preview has **বাংলা ১২৩ / English 123** for the patient-facing parts (directions, duration, steps, follow-up dates and the SMS); **Make this my default** sets the doctor default for new prescriptions (default is Bangla digits). The choice is stored with each issued prescription and the patient view opens with it; the patient can flip it with the toggle there. Clinical values (BP, lab results), Rx number and check code always stay in English digits.
     - Try: Preview -> English 123 and compare the direction and follow-up lines; Patient view -> any Rx -> 123.
   - **Speed layer**: Quick add presets, Apply template (condition templates, saved combinations, single-medicine presets), Save current as template, test templates, Health advice library.
3. **Continue from previous (PHAR-1754)** - banner for returning patients with Continue / View previous / Start new. The drawer shows your own prescriptions as a v1 -> v2 chain with a read-only preview. Continue copies only medicines + advised tests (D2), tags lines "continued", and shows the chip "Continuing from RX-A12345-0042 (v2) - will be saved as v3" with Undo / Start new.
4. **Already on this medicine** - checks each line's generic (lowercased, combinations split on `+`) against the patient's last two issued prescriptions from any doctor plus duplicates inside the current Rx. Continued lines are not warned against their own source but are against the other doctor's Rx. Another doctor's Rx shows generic, strength, frequency, duration, date and "by another doctor" only (D1). Shows ongoing vs "course ended on ...", free-text lines "not checked", the "Checked against: ..." bar. Actions: Keep (acknowledge), Edit dose, Remove. Warnings never block.
   - Try: returning scenario -> Continue -> Napa 500 is flagged against the other doctor's Paracetamol + Caffeine. Add Fexo 120 (Quick add) -> flagged against the other doctor's Fexofenadine. Add Napa Extra -> flagged against both your v2 (course ended 18 Oct) and the other doctor's.
5. **Preview and Complete** - A4-proportion printed layout (doctor header, patient line, Rx no, left notes, right Rx in Bangla frequency format, advice, follow-up, signature, bottom-left QR placeholder + check code). Complete -> confirm summary (warnings acknowledged count, version chain, "previous prescriptions stay unchanged and valid") -> success with new Rx number, version, generated QR placeholder and check code. Print works via the browser.
6. **Verify (public, mobile frame)** - Rx number + check code (Scan QR is simulated). Demo buttons: Valid, Not valid - not found, Not valid - changed, Old Rx (Valid + amber "A newer prescription for this patient exists", D3). A prescription you just issued can be verified too.
7. **Patient view** - customer-portal prescription list and detail (with QR + check code) for Rahima Begum.

Out-of-scope controls show a "Prototype: not built" toast (walk-in registration, completed-visit view).

## Questions for the team

1. **Warning style**: inline card (A), side panel (B), or pop-up (C)? A keeps context but pushes lines down; B gives one summary but needs width; C is hardest to miss but interrupts typing. Hybrid (badge on line + summary) is possible.
2. **Continue banner vs auto-open drawer**: banner is non-intrusive; should View previous open automatically for returning patients?
3. **Section customisation**: per-doctor hide/reorder as built, or per speciality template default only? Should hidden sections still print if filled?
4. **Allergy override** (PHAR-1724): the ticket says issuing needs an override reason; here warnings never block and open ones are only counted on the confirm screen. Block or not?
5. **Pop-up on template/continue**: C currently pops only for single adds and templates, not for Continue (badge + toast instead). Right call?
6. Continue from an older version (drawer): always saved as latest + 1?
7. Check code shown on the printed page only, or also in the patient SMS?
8. **Record write-back** (PHAR-1693): design D8 says always add on issue with no opt-in; the brief for this prototype said "offer to save". Built as D8 (automatic, listed on the confirm screen). Want a per-item opt-out?
9. **Bangla mandatory** (PHAR-1694): free-typed advice without Bangla now blocks Complete. Should library items and free lines also be allowed to be Bangla-only (no English)? Built: yes.
10. **Reminder SMS** (PHAR-1699): who sends it (platform scheduler) and whether the patient can opt out is not in the design doc; built as a per-prescription switch.
